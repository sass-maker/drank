# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

SEO practitioners and Fleet operators who want to monitor Domain Rating trends for public examples and their own domains without creating an account.

## Product Purpose

drank tracks Ahrefs Domain Rating history, highlights movement, and lets users maintain a private browser-local watchlist alongside a shared public comparison set.

## Positioning

Personal domains, history, predictions, and generated advice stay in localStorage; the shared global dataset is public and refreshed independently.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Personal data is browser-local; there is no account or personal-domain database.
- Global comparison data is shared and refreshed weekly.
- Personal refresh is opportunistic while the app is open.
- AI advice must stay grounded in observed rating and trend data.

## Evidence on Hand

Verified product history lives in `PROJECT_STATUS.md`; implementation and shared data live in `app/`, `lib/`, and `data/`.

## Product Principles

- Keep personal tracking local.
- Distinguish observed rating data from general advice.
- Make freshness and limits visible.
- Keep planned work in GitHub Issues.

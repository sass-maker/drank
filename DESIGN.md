# Design

## Direction

Preserve drank’s near-black measurement dashboard: Geist typography, white primary controls, emerald data accents, compact monospace labels, and spacious chart-oriented composition.

## Rules

- New routes inherit the dashboard header, zinc surfaces, and restrained borders.
- Use emerald only for trustworthy metadata and positive data context.
- Keep personal-data boundaries visible in the footer or close.
- Reading surfaces use simple chronology rather than dashboard cards.
